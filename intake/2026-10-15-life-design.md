## A good week

Gym twice, daughter drop-off once, 30–45 minutes with the kids after work, dog walk three times, golf once, series at night. Break 17:00–19:30, then an hour or so more if he has to, done by 20:00–20:30. "Problem is I am always checking if something has come through and never leave a day with even one unread email, ever."

## The work he'd keep

Searching artists, listening to music, checking who tours where, artist stats, reaching out to agents to book them, tour financial models, festival properties and annual recurring concepts, creative concept work, big brand activations.

## Off his desk by Christmas

Mushroom Motion admin (WhatsApps and emails he's cc'd on). Music Republik admin. All finance and accounts for Music Republik and Merchhaus. Merchhaus forecasts, ordering and settlements, handed to "the two youngsters." Marketing plan, on-sale process, concept decks, brand-partner outreach. Quotes and lead gen. "A lot of automation."

## A Tuesday in three years

Gym, breakfast with his wife. A few major annual music events and recurring-revenue projects, a few clients, 4–5 staff max, R4–5m a year. No merchandise forecasts or show operations. No involvement in the camera business "and tons of admin as a 12% shareholder." 100% his own businesses with his own infrastructure: own ticketing platform, own marketing team, own operations people. "I just need to listen and find good artists, keep brands happy and put on spectacular shows."

## Non-negotiables

- The family trust, which owns 100% of his investment holding company, is never part of any deal. No sureties. Nothing from co-shareholders reaches it.
- Added on the call: stay complementary to Big Concerts, never a competitor. Family (his father; Justin is CEO/chairman). The in-house merch position at Big Concerts' new 12,000-capacity dome venue (venue takes about 30% of merch gross) is a prize to protect.
- Added on the call, the "no perimeter": music only. "Sport is not me. I'm Music Republik." Sixes cricket/golf festival, boxing, anything outside music gets a no.

## Purpose, in his words

"What's the point in bringing an already super wealthy artist that you overpay to come to South Africa, with their egos and the greedy agents? Is it even worth it?" Better purpose: building local artists, giving them a backbone, putting shows on for them, exporting them. "I had a recording studio. I recorded music. Getting close to music again is what I love."

Johannes's frame, accepted on the call: not an exit business. A cash-flow business that takes care of the family, with the most time and flexibility and the least stress. Health, family, wealth, in that order.
