```
  project knowledge. [Confirm which artists can be named publicly.]

- Keep MerchHaus out of it unless asked. That's a separate project.
```

```

Town. Two lines of business:
1. Tour merch fulfilment for international artists and major events in South

2. B2B corporate apparel and brand activations.

How to work in here:

  income share or royalty.
- For corporate clients, write in plain business language: what they get,
  when, and what it costs.
- Write in Julien's voice (julien-voice skill).
- Keep Music Republik advisory work out of it unless asked.
```
