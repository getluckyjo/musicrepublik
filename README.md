## Working rules

- Life design first. Every project, opportunity and deliverable is scored against `intake/2026-10-15-life-design.md`.
- Hours, never days. The seat is 10 hours a month.
- Numbers are Julien's own. Where a number is a draft or an estimate it is marked as such.
- Nothing in this repo goes to Julien without Johannes's review.
