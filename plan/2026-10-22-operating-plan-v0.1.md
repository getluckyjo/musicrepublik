# Music Republik · Operating plan v0.1

Draft, 22 Oct 2026. For Johannes's review before anything goes to Julien. Built from the life design (`intake/2026-10-15-life-design.md`), the 22 Oct intake call transcript and the emails and spreadsheets to date. Every judgement below is a draft.

## 0. How the plan works

Five layers. Each one feeds the next, and each one is a panel on the dashboard.

1. **Life design** is the scoreboard. Targets, limits, non-negotiables. Nothing else moves unless it moves these.
2. **Decision filter** turns the life design into a yes or no for every opportunity, and a decision log so the same question is never argued twice.
3. **Focus** names the lines that get Julien's hours this quarter. Everything else gets automated, delegated, held or parked.
4. **Resource allocation** gives each focus line its hours, cash and people for 90 days.
5. **Deliverables** are dated, owned, have a done-definition, and each one emits a number the dashboard tracks.

The rhythm: Tuesday 11:00 weekly call (Julien's proposed slot). Before the call, the dashboard numbers update. On the call: what moved, what's stuck, decisions logged, next week's list. Every 90 days: review against the life design, re-cut focus and allocation, bump the plan version.

The overarching goal for the first 90 days, in Johannes's words on the call: **create momentum**. Julien is stuck because four months of effort on international acts produced nothing. The plan swaps that for things he controls.

## 2. Decision filter

Six gates. An opportunity needs at least four, and gate 4 is never waived.

1. Is it music?
2. Does it build the local scene, a local artist, or a platform Julien owns?
3. Is Julien uniquely positioned (SA live experience, Big Concerts network, his data)?
4. Capital at risk inside the R1.5m ceiling, and nothing touches the trust or needs a surety?
5. After setup, does it run without him? (Automated, delegated, or a partner carries it.)
6. Complementary to Big Concerts?

## 6. The dashboard this becomes

**Panels:** Scoreboard (life design tiles, target vs actual). Money (cash, runway, committed, capital at risk, pipeline). Lines (one card per revenue line: status focus/contain/park, 12-month revenue and profit, hours a week, enjoyment, bottleneck, next deliverable). Decision log. Deliverables (owner, due, status, number). Weekly check-ins (hours, evenings off, enjoyment, what moved).

**Sources, in the order they get connected:** weekly call inputs (hours, evenings, enjoyment) → accounting (Music Republik and Merchhaus, monthly) → Yoco and the shop (merch sales per event) → Chartmetric (artist data) → Composio inbox stats (mail load) → Fastgate (tickets, fan database).

**Build path:** v0 in November, one page updated by hand before each call. v1 in December, a shared page with its own database so Julien can update his tiles himself. v2 in Q1 2027, live connectors.
