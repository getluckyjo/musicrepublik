Revive the Republic: own-platform nights, JohannesburgGo · focus

Passes all six. Low capital, high purpose, builds the brand and the fan base, feeds ticketing, merch and streaming.

Fastgate ticketingGo · the rail

WhatsApp-native, promoter-friendly, fan database. For his own events first. Not a software company this quarter. Pre-sales are never the risk budget.

Local-artist merch platformBuild

Chartmetric shows acts with hundreds of millions of views and no merch. Automated from day one. Starts after J. Cole.

Big Concerts in-house merch at the new dome venueProtect

The venue takes about 30% of merch gross on every promoter's show. Worth more than any single international act.

Add-on products at showsYes

Power banks, earplugs, posters, vinyl bundles. 100% margin, no artist share. Test at J. Cole.

International act offersData only

James Arthur 2027, Daughtry, Chris Tomlin 2028; Cat Stevens declined at $350k. $300–350k for two shows breaks gate four even at 50/50. Open offers stay open passively. An agent sends offers to build the price database. A deal proceeds only when the model shows profit inside the ceiling with a co-promoter.

Mushroom Motion retainerEnforce and sign

R20k a month plus 20% profit share, 12% stake. The unsigned v3 agreement already caps the retainer at 12 hours, with R1,750 an hour beyond it and 20% of net profit on clients he brings. Actual hours are 60–70. Sign it with the cap at four hours, enforce the notification clause from November, invoice the overage. Exit the retainer if they refuse.

Old Mutual private wealth referralKeep, don't build

30% of the fee, R13k a month. Passive income that counts toward the run-rate. The 400–500 high-net-worth campaign is not music and fails gate one.

Artist agency and booking systemHold

Sign 10–30 local acts at 25%. Right idea. Needs the platform and the first show first.

Tarly, fan-request-an-artist platformHold

Music and local, but not his platform. Revisit after Revive #1.

Corporate apparel at MerchhausPark

Commodity, wins on price, "a pain." Off the website.

Merch Showroom sub-licensingPark

WWE and the like. Needs a retail licence and capital. "2028."

Sixes cricket/golf festival, boxing, any sportNo

Not music. "Sport is not me. I'm Music Republik."
