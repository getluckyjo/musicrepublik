How the plan works

Four months of offers to international acts produced nothing. This swaps that for things Julien controls, and makes every week measurable.

1
Life designThe scoreboard. Targets, limits, non-negotiables. Nothing else moves unless it moves these.
2
Decision filterSix gates and a decision log. Nothing gets argued twice.
3
FocusThree lines get his hours this quarter. The rest: automate, hold or park.
4
ResourcesHours, cash and people per line, per 90 days.
5
DeliverablesDated, owned, a done-definition, and a number for the dashboard.
The rhythm

Tuesday 11:00 weekly call, Julien's proposed slot. Numbers update before the call. On the call: what moved, what's stuck, decisions logged, next week's list. Every 90 days: review against the life design, re-cut focus and allocation, bump the version.

Decision filterdraft

An opportunity needs at least four of six. Gate four is never waived.

1Is it music?
2Does it build the local scene, a local artist, or a platform he owns?
3Is he uniquely positioned: SA live experience, the Big Concerts network, his data?
4Capital inside the R1.5m ceiling, nothing near the trust, no surety.
5After setup, does it run without him?
6Complementary to Big Concerts?

The dashboard this becomes

This page is v0. Each section above is already a panel; the numbers start flowing in as the sources connect.

Panels
  • Scoreboard. Life-design tiles, target versus actual.
  • Money. Cash, runway, committed, capital at risk, pipeline.
  • Lines. One card per revenue line: status, 12-month revenue and profit, hours a week, enjoyment, bottleneck, next deliverable.
  • Decision log. Every yes and no, with the gates it passed.
  • Deliverables. Owner, due, status, number.
  • Weekly check-ins. Hours, evenings off, enjoyment, what moved.
Sources, in connection order
  • Weekly call inputs: hours, evenings, enjoyment.
  • Accounting for Music Republik and Merchhaus, monthly.
  • Yoco and the shop: merch sales per event.
  • Chartmetric: artist data.
  • Composio inbox stats: mail load.
  • Fastgate: tickets and the fan database.
