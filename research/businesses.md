### Site audit: what I'd raise

- **Stock photos where his real shows should be.** The "From Concept to Crowd"
  gallery's first row is Unsplash stock. He's run Metallica and U2. Real show
  photos (rights permitting) would carry far more weight.
- **Promises merch, shows no merch.** The meta description says "deliver full
  merchandise solutions" and the keywords include "tour merchandise", but the page
  has no merch section and no link to MerchHaus.
- **Big numbers, no names.** "30+ artists promoted" is on the site, but the
  artists aren't named there, though they are on LinkedIn. Ask whether that's
  deliberate (Big Concerts' shows, not his) or just missing.
- **Blog has gone quiet.** Six posts, all signed "Music Republik Team". Four
  are dated 24 May 2026 and two March 2025. Nothing in four months. The
  March 2025 dates predate the business (per LinkedIn), so check they're right.
- **"View Our Work" looks unlinked** in the scrape. Click it to check.
- **Phone number only appears in a blog footer**, not on the contact page. No
  WhatsApp, though SA corporates will want it.

## Coaching angles: hypotheses to test, not conclusions

1. **Two businesses, one founder, five months in.** Show days need teams of
   6–50; promotion is deal-heavy and capital-heavy; advisory is cross-border.
   Where does his week actually go, and where does the next six months' money
   come from?
2. **Lumpy revenue.** Tour merch follows the tour calendar. Promotion carries
   real capital risk. Advisory is project fees. **Corporate apparel is the
   only line that can be steady and recurring.** Is that the base load, and is it
   being sold on purpose?
3. **One front door or two?** His LinkedIn headline merges both
   ("Promoter & Merchandiser"). The Music Republik site ignores MerchHaus.
   MerchHaus has no site. Who is the customer for each, and do they overlap?
4. **"Independent" vs Live Nation.** Music Republik says it "operates
   independently and without affiliation to any venue group or ticketing
   platform". MerchHaus is Big Concerts' merch partner, and Music Republik
   promotes shows in the same market. Is the relationship an asset, a conflict,
   or both? How do agents read it?
5. **The numbers guy without a dashboard?** "Data before commitment" is his
   brand. Where does he see cash, pipeline and per-show merch performance across
   both companies today? Probably spreadsheets, which makes a dashboard or tool
   an easy win.
6. **Two voices.** His LinkedIn posts are human and reflective; the site is
   polished agency copy. The posts are the stronger asset. They're the reason
   someone would pick a five-month-old business over an incumbent.
7. **The pressure is real.** He's said so publicly. Treat that as an opening
   to talk about it, not a weakness.
