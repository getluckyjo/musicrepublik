# Julien van Wyk: the person

**Live entertainment promoter and merchandiser who is also a qualified management
accountant.** About 15 years inside South Africa's biggest promoter, now about four
to five months into running his own two businesses. LinkedIn tagline: *"data
before commitment, disciplined margins, and forecasts grounded in reality
rather than optimism."*

## Credentials

- **CIMA / CGMA**: Chartered Global Management Accountant (2012–2017)
- **BAcc**: Stellenbosch University (2007–2010)
- **Diploma in Sound Engineering**: Cape Audio College (2010–2011)
- Bishops Diocesan College, matric 2006
- LinkedIn top skills: Business Analysis, Financial Analysis, Pricing Strategy

He's an accountant and a musician. He co-produced, sang and wrote for Audio
Engine, a Cape Town electronic act (2014–2018), and co-owned Frequency Farm
Studios, an audio post-production and recording studio (2013–2018).
